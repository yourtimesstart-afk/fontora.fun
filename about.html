🎨 What is Fontora?

Fontora is a free font discovery and download platform where anyone can browse, preview, and instantly download high-quality typefaces — no sign-up, no paywalls, no restrictions. Every font in our collection is 100% free for personal and commercial use.

We started Fontora with a simple mission: make beautiful typography accessible to everyone, everywhere — regardless of language, budget, or location. Whether you're designing a wedding invitation in Gujarat, building an app in Tokyo, or setting type in Cairo, the perfect font should be just one click away.

💡 807+ fonts across 40+ languages — including 9 Indian languages (ગુજરાતી, हिंदी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, বাংলা, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, ଓଡ଼ିଆ), plus Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Khmer, Cyrillic, Greek, Hebrew, and many more.

📚 Our Font Collection

Our library is carefully organized into categories for every creative need:

  • 💒 Wedding & Calligraphy (99 fonts) — elegant scripts like Great Vibes, Dancing Script, Allura, Parisienne, Cormorant, and Style Script
  • 🇮🇳 Indian Languages (174 fonts) — Noto, Anek, and Baloo families for all 9 major Indian scripts, plus Tiro Devanagari, Hind Vadodara, Mukta Vaani, and more
  • 🌏 East Asian (53 fonts) — Noto Sans/Serif for Chinese, Japanese, and Korean, plus ZCOOL, Klee One, Zen Gothic, Nanum, and Black Han Sans
  • 🕌 Arabic & Hebrew (33 fonts) — Amiri, Cairo, Tajawal, Reem Kufi, Scheherazade, Frank Ruhl Libre, and Markazi Text with full RTL support
  • 🌴 Southeast Asian (57 fonts) — Thai (Kanit, Chakra Petch), Khmer (Noto, Kantumruy, Hanuman), Lao, and Myanmar fonts
  • 🔤 Latin & Cyrillic (100+ fonts) — Inter, Manrope, Roboto, PT Sans/Serif, Playfair Display, Montserrat, Bebas Neue, and many more

⚖️ Licensing & Font Sources

All fonts on Fontora are open-source and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 or Apache License 2.0. You can freely:

  • ✅ Use them in personal projects (homework, invitations, personal websites)
  • ✅ Use them in commercial projects (logos, products, advertisements, client work)
  • ✅ Modify and redistribute them
  • ✅ Embed them in websites and apps

Fonts are sourced from the Google Fonts open-source repository and the Noto fonts project by Google. We do not host or distribute any proprietary or pirated fonts.

Fontora is designed, developed, and maintained by Mukeshkumar Parmar, a developer and designer based in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. Passionate about typography, open-source software, and making technology accessible across languages.

The project was built with a vision to celebrate India's linguistic diversity while connecting designers worldwide with the global open-source type community. Every aspect — from the geolocation-based language detection to the 9 Indian script categories — reflects that mission.

🚀 Technology

Fontora is built with pure, lightweight web technologies — no heavy frameworks, no build step required:

  • HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript — fast and dependency-free
  • Font Loading API — fonts load lazily as you scroll
  • Geolocation API — auto-detects visitor's country for language
  • LocalStorage — remembers your language preference
  • All fonts hosted locally — works offline once loaded
