Frequently Asked Questions

📥 Are all fonts really free?

Yes! Every font on Fontora is 100% free for both personal and commercial use under the SIL Open Font License or Apache License 2.0.

🌍 Can I request a specific font or language?

Absolutely! Send us a message with the font name or language you'd like to see, and we'll do our best to add it to the library.

🐛 I found a bug or broken font

Please let us know! Email us with the font name and a description of the issue, and we'll fix it as soon as possible.

🤝 Can I contribute fonts?

If you're a type designer with open-source fonts, we'd love to feature your work. Reach out with your font details.

📋 Can I use fonts for client work?

Yes! All fonts can be used in commercial projects including logos, websites, advertisements, and client deliverables.

📱 Does the website work offline?

Once fonts are loaded in your browser, previews work offline. Font downloads require an internet connection on first visit.
